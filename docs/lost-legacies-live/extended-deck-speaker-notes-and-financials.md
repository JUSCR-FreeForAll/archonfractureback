## Slide 15 — Unit Economics: Hypotheses to Prove
- Shared infrastructure may reduce incremental product launch costs; measure engineering hours and third-party spend per launch.
- Cross-product movement may improve lifetime value; measure consented activation, retention, and paid conversion across products.
- Local-first execution may reduce server load; benchmark comparable workloads and include device costs, sync traffic, support, and security operations.
- Platform reuse may improve gross margin; report gross margin by product and shared service allocation.

**Speaker notes:** These are unit-economics hypotheses, not established outcomes. We will instrument a baseline and compare actual cost-to-serve, contribution margin, retention, and cross-product adoption.

**Visual prompt:** Three-column hypothesis dashboard labeled Launch Cost, Customer Lifetime Value, and Cost to Serve. Each column shows a metric placeholder and “baseline required” tag. Dark navy, gold accents, no invented metric values.
